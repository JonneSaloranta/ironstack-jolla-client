# Changelog

What changed in each version of IronStack for Sailfish OS. The version
is the repo-root `VERSION` file; bumping it on master publishes a
release (docs/RELEASING.md). A release's notes on GitHub are this file's
section for it, followed by every commit since the previous release.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-04

- Use the app without a server: "Use without a server" on the login page
  keeps everything on the phone. Training works as with a server -
  IronStack's exercises and program templates, your own programs and
  exercises, training mode with weight suggestions, records and their
  notification, history, Progress and achievements. Body tracking,
  activities, stretching and nutrition come to this mode later.
- What needs a server is shown dimmed there, with what it would do;
  Settings can hide it, and backs up the phone's data as a JSON file.

## [0.9.1] - 2026-11-04

- An AI assistant suggestion of a kind the app doesn't know yet is shown
  with a note to create it on the web, not as a diet plan.

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-04

- With IronStack's next release: images of your own exercises from the
  gallery, a "this week" summary, how long a workout takes and the time
  left in training mode, becoming a trainer in the app, and recipes the
  AI assistant suggests. Hidden or explained with IronStack 1.31.
- Asks for the Pictures and Media index permissions, for the gallery.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-04

- An app log under Settings > Troubleshooting: errors, warnings, info or
  debug, copied or shared to report a problem.
- Server names and texts follow the phone's language even when its
  region format is another one.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-04

- Needs IronStack 1.31.0 or later.
- Built-in names and texts come translated from IronStack, in the
  phone's language.
- The cool-down suggestion, nutrition statistics and observed
  maintenance come from IronStack: fewer requests, no whole-diary reads.

## [0.6.3] - 2026-11-04

- The barcode scanner's torch lights up.

## [0.6.2] - 2026-11-04

- The barcode scanner's viewfinder is upright.

## [0.6.1] - 2026-11-03

- Lists longer than one page work behind a reverse proxy that gives the
  server another address (the diary said "Refused to follow a link to
  another server").

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-03

- Scan a food's barcode with the camera.
- In Swedish, Estonian, Italian and Russian too.
- The dashboard's "Recently active" list.
- A coach's update shows what it changes first.
- A coach's shared programs are no longer listed as your own.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-03

- Friends and groups: requests, invites, conversations, blocking and
  group management.
- Coaching: ask a trainer, import and update shared plans; a trainer's
  clients and sharing.
- The AI assistant: conversations, suggested plans, its settings.
- The web's guided tours as a Guide.
- A deleted diet plan's shopping list ticks are forgotten; the binary
  is stripped.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-03

- Needs IronStack 1.30.0 or later.
- Food diary totals from the logged values, built-in recipes, copy day
  and food search on the server.
- Diary entries, body measurements, activities and stretching are saved
  on the phone without a connection and sent later.
- Nutrition goals set and calorie adjustments accepted in the app.
- Open Food Facts search and import through IronStack.
- Guided stretching sessions on the server, and a cool-down suggested
  after a finished workout.
- Progress page and strength trend from IronStack's analytics.
- The search fields keep the keyboard open while typing.

## [0.3.1] - 2026-11-03

- Nutrition statistics table and the dashboard's body weight fit with
  large text sizes.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-03

- Cover: today's calories and protein against the target, and a quick
  entry action, when no workout is on.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-03

- Progress page: training summary, body changes, strength trend, weekly
  and muscle group volume, personal records.
- Nutrition statistics, "Is your current target working?" and observed
  maintenance; Open Food Facts search by name or barcode.
- Body tracking reminder; stretching timer sounds (Audio permission).
- Body tracking, activities, stretching and nutrition like the web.
- Fixes: diary meals and days, built-in and deleted recipes in the diary,
  new items opening their page, shopping list ticks kept.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-23

- First version: programs, workouts, set logging, history and records.
